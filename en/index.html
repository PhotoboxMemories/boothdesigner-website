Frequently asked questions

How do I add the studio to my website?

In the dashboard you get a link and an embed code. You paste the code into any website (WordPress, Wix, Jimdo, Shopware …). Example:

<iframe src="https://boothdesigner.de/studio/?b=your-name" style="width:100%;height:90vh;border:0" title="Design your photo booth layout"></iframe>
Do I need the Photobox Memories photo booth software?

No. You can download every submission as a print-ready PNG with transparent photo fields and use it as an overlay in dslrBooth, Breeze Booth, Sparkbooth or any other photo booth software. With Photobox Memories the transfer takes just one click.

Can my customers download the designs themselves?

Not by default – they send the design to you. If you like, you can enable downloads in the dashboard.

Who owns the designs?

The designs are part of the subscription. You may use them for your photo booth jobs as long as your subscription is active. Reselling the design files as your own templates is not permitted.

What happens after the trial?

The trial ends automatically after 14 days. You then decide in the dashboard whether to continue monthly or yearly. There are no costs if you don't book anything.

Which occasions are there photo booth templates for?

For weddings, birthdays, parties, summer parties, graduations and school starts, hen and stag parties, baby showers, christenings, communions and confirmations, company parties, Christmas and winter as well as New Year's Eve, Halloween, carnival and Easter. Over 400 photo booth layouts in total – and new ones are added all the time.

Which formats and print sizes are available?

Every design comes as a 2×6 inch photo strip, 4×6 portrait, 6×4 landscape, 5×5 square and as a magazine cover – print-ready at 300 dpi, with a matching start screen for the photo booth.

How do I create a photo booth layout?

Choose a template, enter names and date, adjust colours and fonts – done. The photo booth layout is created right in the browser, on a phone or computer, and saved as a print-ready template at 300 dpi. With the Photobox Memories software, the layout lands in the event with one click.

What is a photo booth template?

A photo booth template is the layout into which the photo booth places the guests' photos – with background, decoration, texts and photo areas. BoothDesigner has over 400 templates that your customers customise themselves.
